Evaluate Patchwork as if it were a final submission competing for a prize. Read the product, README, implementation, and submission guidance. Score each judging dimension from 1–10, cite evidence, identify the three highest-leverage improvements, and flag any claim the demo cannot prove.

Be demanding, specific, and fair. Do not edit the repository.
